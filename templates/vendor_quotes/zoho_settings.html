How quotations are created

  • Always as a draft; nothing is sent to the customer.
  • Lines are typed fresh on the quotation (part no., description, qty, unit selling price in IDR); nothing is added to the Zoho item list.
  • Reference = the ENQ number. New quotations use Zoho's own auto-number (FFI_…).
  • Revisions get the original number + R1, R2… (e.g. FFI_26000278R1) as a new draft.
