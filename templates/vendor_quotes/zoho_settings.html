How quotations are created

  • Always as a draft in the Zoho Books of the quote's company; nothing is sent to the customer.
  • Lines are typed fresh on the quotation; nothing is added to the Zoho item list. Reference = the ENQ number.
  • FFI: Zoho's own auto-number (FFI_…). FFE: the workspace takes the next number after the highest FFE_YY… in FFE Zoho Books; a new year starts at FFE_YY000001.
  • Revisions get the original number + R1, R2… (e.g. FFE_26000081R1) as a new draft.
  • Connecting checks the Zoho login really has that company's organisation, so FFI and FFE can't be mixed up.
